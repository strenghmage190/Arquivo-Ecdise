## Verification

### Must Haves
- The center preview uses the full-height `ProfessionalSpectrogram` component.
- The `SpectrogramPreviewCanvas` accurately overlays the base spectrogram using `mix-blend-mode`.

- Visual parity with `InspectionModal.tsx` is achieved.

### Manual Testing Steps
1. Open "Laboratório de Áudio".
2. Add a base audio file. Verify `ProfessionalSpectrogram` renders it cleanly with a thin timeline waveform at the top.
3. Switch to the "Texto Oculto" or "Imagem Oculta" tab and add a payload.
4. Verify the payload's frequencies blend (`screen`/`lighten`) over the existing spectrogram.
