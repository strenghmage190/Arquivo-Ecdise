## Goal

Refactor `UVEditor.css` and related UI controls to match the Cyberpunk / Nexus theme tokens, replace emoji labels in the RGB channel selectors with glowing cyber badges, and establish consistent button sizing and hover feedback.

## Target Files

- `src/components/tools/UVEditor.css`
- `src/components/tools/UVEditor.tsx`
- `src/components/LayersPanel.css` (or `UVEditor.css` layers section)
- `src/components/LayerItem.tsx`

## Tasks

### Task 1: Cyberpunk RGB Channel Badge Overhaul
- In `src/components/tools/UVEditor.tsx`:
  - Replace `'🔴 Red'`, `'🟢 Green'`, `'🔵 Blue'` and `'🎯 Canal Alvo RGB'` with sleek cyberpunk badges with channel indicators (`R`, `G`, `B`).

  - Polish properties panel inputs, range sliders (`.uv-range`), and color picker preview.

### Task 3: Layer Row Styling and Hover Actions Polish
- In `src/components/LayerItem.tsx` and `src/components/tools/UVEditor.css`:
